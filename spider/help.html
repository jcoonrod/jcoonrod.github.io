  • One-click Play: You only click a card once to move it. It will send it to the first logical destination. To move it to the next logical destination, click it again. This includes dropping from the foundation if you didn't want it there.
  • Decks: This games uses two decks. It deals the first 54 down and the remaining 50 can be deal ten at a time.
  • Tableau building: Stack cards in descending order (King down to Ace) using any color.
  • Moving stacks: Move multi-card face-up sequences together if all the cards are of the same suit.
  • Empty spaces: Any card or moveable group can go to an empty space.
  • Foundation building: Once you have a g.
  • Stock pile:Click on the Stock pile to flip card three at a time.
