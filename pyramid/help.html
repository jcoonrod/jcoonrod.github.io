Go Back

  • Goal: Make all the cards disappear from bottom up.
  • Play: Cards have values 1 to 13, Ace to King. You can only disappear cards that aren't covered by others. If you click on a King, it will disappear. Otherwise you need to click on two cards that add up to 13.
  • Stock pile:Click on the Stock pile to flip one card.
