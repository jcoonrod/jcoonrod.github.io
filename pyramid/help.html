  • One-click Play: You only click a card once to move it. It will send it to the first logical destination. To move it to the next logical destination, click it again. This includes dropping from the foundation if you didn't want it there.
  • Tableau building: Stack cards in descending order (King down to Ace) using alternating colors (red on black, black on red).
  • Moving groups: Move multi-card face-up sequences together if the bottom card of the stack fits the alternating-color and descending-rank rule.
  • Empty spaces: Only a King (or a stack starting with a King) can fill an empty tableau column.
  • Foundation building: Move Aces to the foundation as soon as they appear, then stack cards of the same suit in ascending order (Ace to King).
  • Stock pile:Click on the Stock pile to flip card three at a time.
